Why the solution can wander. Three variables supply six variances and covariances, but a two-factor model has eight free parameters (six loadings and three uniquenesses, less one for rotation). That leaves df = −2, so many different solutions reproduce the correlations equally well. Principal axis factoring still returns one, but its communalities often climb toward 1 (a Heywood case). Switch to principal components to see how much the answer depends on the method, and switch rotations to see that every solution lies in the same plane.
